What is BMI?

BMI compares weight with height. It is a screening measure, not a direct measurement of body fat or overall health.

Daily Calories

Your calorie needs depend on body size, age, activity, health, and changes in body weight over time.

Stay Consistent

Combine balanced nutrition, regular exercise, sleep, and gradual adjustments to support your fitness goals.

Note: These calculations are estimates for adults, not medical advice. BMI may not accurately reflect body composition, especially for very muscular people. Calorie needs vary between individuals.
